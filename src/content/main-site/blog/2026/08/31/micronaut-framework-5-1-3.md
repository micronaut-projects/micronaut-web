We are excited to announce the release of Micronaut framework 5.1.3! It includes a release of [Core](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-core/releases/tag/v5.1.13), [SQL](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-sql/releases/tag/v7.1.2), [Micrometer](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-micrometer/releases/tag/v6.0.2), [OpenAPI](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-openapi/releases/tag/v7.1.3), [OpenSearch](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-opensearch/releases/tag/v2.1.1), [Email](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-email/releases/tag/v3.2.0), [Reactor](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-reactor/releases/tag/v4.2.0), [Data](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-data/releases/tag/v5.1.3), and [AWS](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-aws/releases/tag/v5.1.1).

If you haven’t yet updated to [Micronaut Framework 5](https://micronaut.io/2026/05/20/micronaut-framework-5-0-0-released/), this is an excellent opportunity to do so!

Please feel free to [reach out to us](https://micronaut.io/support/) if you need any assistance.
