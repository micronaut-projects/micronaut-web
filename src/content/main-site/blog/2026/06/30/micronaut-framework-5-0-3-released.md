We are excited to announce the release of Micronaut framework 5.0.3! It includes releases of [Micronaut Core](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-core/releases/tag/v5.0.4), [Micronaut AWS](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-aws/releases/tag/v5.0.1), [Micronaut Data](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-data/releases/tag/v5.0.5), [Micronaut SQL](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-sql/releases/tag/v7.0.2), [Micronaut R2DBC](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-r2dbc/releases/tag/v7.0.2), [Micronaut Problem](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-problem/releases/tag/v5.0.0), [Micronaut Test](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-test/releases/tag/v5.0.1), and [Micronaut Micrometer](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-micrometer/releases/tag/v6.0.1).

If you haven’t yet updated to [Micronaut Framework 5](https://micronaut.io/2026/05/20/micronaut-framework-5-0-0-released/), this is an excellent opportunity to do so!

Please feel free to [reach out to us](https://micronaut.io/support/) if you need any assistance.
